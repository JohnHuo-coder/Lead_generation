---
name: amenities
description: Use when researching on-site gym, spa, pool, fitness, or wellness facilities.
---

## researcher
- On-site gym, spa, pool, fitness, or wellness must be the property's own
  facility. Nearby, partner, or mall facilities do not count unless the
  requirement says they do.

## selector
- Prefer official amenities / facilities / wellness / spa / pool / gym
  pages, or an official PDF fact sheet that lists facilities; then
  hotel-group official property pages that list on-site facilities.
- If an official facilities URL is a candidate, select it over the
  marketing homepage or an OTA guest-room page.
- Skip sibling properties and different-city locations.

## extract
- Extract named on-site facilities when the source attributes them to the
  target property. Omit vague photos with no facility name or location.
