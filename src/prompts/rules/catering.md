---
name: catering
description: Use when researching on-site group catering, banquet service, or event food and beverage.
---

## researcher
- Judge only the catering condition. Meeting-room capacity is not evidence
  for this requirement.
- Catering or banquet menus and event food-and-beverage prices can establish
  the matching part of the condition. Do not wait for wording such as
  "on-site catering team" or "own kitchen" unless the requirement asks for
  that distinction.
- Guest-restaurant dining or "no restaurant" is not event catering.
- A meetings-page catering menu is evidence of group catering unless the
  source says clients must hire an outside caterer.

## query
- Prefer keywords for catering, banquet, or event dining. An official
  domain or event-page query is enough; do not switch the topic to
  meeting-room capacity.

## selector
- Prefer official meeting/event pages, banquet kits, catering menus, or
  official PDFs that list event F&B; then Cvent or other venue listings
  that state catering or banquet service.
- If an official events or catering URL is a candidate, select it over
  the homepage. Skip sibling properties and different-city locations.

## extract
- Extract catering or banquet menus, event F&B prices, and statements that
  the property provides or does not provide group catering.
- Do not treat cleanliness, COVID, or generic "third-party partners"
  boilerplate as catering evidence.
- Do not treat guest-restaurant dining as group event catering.
- Do not extract meeting-room capacities for this requirement.
